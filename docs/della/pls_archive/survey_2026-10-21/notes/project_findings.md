## Q1. What has been established, with which figure/run and numbers?

### Takeaway
On the Marin MuonH Qwen3 recipe, a next-token loss at every layer (separate heads) makes every intermediate layer a decent early-exit predictor but leaves each layer 0.07–0.17 nats (6 layers) or 0.07–0.10 nats (48 layers, at 12/24/36) above an ordinary model of that depth, and costs the last layer ~0.09–0.11 nats in eight of ten DepthBench designs; the cost lives in the backbone features, not the heads, and no fix that keeps every layer supervised removes it. The newer "internals" work shows that separate heads put middle layers into the output head's coordinates, make 13–28 of 48 layers nearly free to delete, remove the ordinary model's last-MLP anti-correction, and align AttnRes pseudo-queries with the head; Kimi K3 (Block AttnRes, 93 layers) instead forms its prediction in the last ~15 layers and writes oversized, head-avoiding vectors at block ends.

- Mechanism of the cost at 6 layers (in-training dynamics): supervised arms' in-context score lags the baseline by 30–50 steps (baseline drops −0.13 → −0.28 at steps 150–200; arms catch up ~step 290); the crossover where the supervised final layer becomes worse is ~step 140–160 (~3% of training); shallow layers stay predictors (logit-lens ICL −0.07 to −0.17) whereas baseline shallow layers turn into feature suppliers — [PLSMD 诊断结果](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).
- Switching per-layer supervision off before the in-context onset (shared head; off at step 80, step 140, or LLAL-style linear 1→0 over 0–150) ends at the baseline pool: +0.0003 / +0.0006 / +0.0006 bpb, no lasting gain; the transient advantage peaks at −0.0052 bpb at step 2000 (off80) — [PLSMD 神谕切换结果](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).
- Read-only shared head (stop-grad into head) does not reduce the cost, so the damage is to the backbone, not the shared head being pulled; separate heads remove only ~1/6 (≈0.007 bpb) of the cost — [PLSMD](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).
- Heads vs features: freezing each finished 300m backbone and retraining heads 2,000 more steps (fresh optimizer, LR rewarmed to peak) finds no better head at any layer; sep heads end slightly higher, probes barely move and stay far above sep at early layers → the gap is in the features — [PAGE Fig 18 + body](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html).

#### Theme B: fixes tried (all negative at the "every layer low" criterion)
- Owner criterion (2026-10-05): progress only if a setup beats separate heads at weight 1 (mean per-layer gap 0.107) at every layer; weight 0.2 gives mean 0.129 — [MEM](/home/USER/.claude/projects/-home-USER/memory/project_pls_per_layer_supervision_closed.md).
- Weight sweep (1, 0.2, 0.1, 0.05) × gradient reach (all layers below vs own layer only): each step down in weight brings the last layer closer and the first layers further; stopping gradient at the own layer does not help and at weight 1 makes every layer worse; weight 0.05 gives L1 0.394, L6 0.021 nats — [PAGE Fig 12 + body](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html).
- Depth-rising weights (layer k gets k/15) give the lowest last layer and worst first two layers; per-token gradient surgery (PCGrad-style, at 0.2 and at 1) changes every layer only a little; batch-level cosine between layer-loss and final-loss gradients is already positive — [PAGE Figs 13–14](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html).
- In-run gradient cosine (aux vs final) stays 0.8–0.9 throughout and cannot detect the crossover; in the baseline the counterfactual cosine is ~0.1 (L0 ~0.01) — [PLSMD](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).

#### Theme D: Hyper-Connections pays half (hidden-write account)
- Post-hoc theory (2026-10-14): a design pays less only if a layer can write a feature no readout sees; HC readout = sum of 4 streams and its writes are signed, so zero-sum writes are invisible; mHC writes are positive with non-negative mixing. Explicitly flagged as post hoc on all 8 designs; MoDA (gap .103) was not a blind test — [PLSMD](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).
- State-level prediction FAILED: zero-sum share of stream energy (mean L1–47): HC sep 0.26, HC probes 0.59, mHC 0.35/0.36. Write-level version fits post hoc: sublayers with static write >80% zero-sum: HC sep 10/96 (layers 4–14, 19), HC probes 1, mHC 0 — [PLSMD](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).
- Ablation (replace every stream by the stream mean after layer c; L48 CE rise at c=4/8/12/16/20/28/36/44): HC sep +0.65/+1.12/+1.07/+0.45/+0.24/+0.07/+0.02/0; HC probes +2.67/+1.40/+0.56/+1.13/+2.24/+2.38/+0.10/+0.04; mHC sep +0.21/+0.29/+0.22/+0.14/+0.11/+0.06/+0.01/0; mHC probes +0.06…+0.23/+0.09/+0.10 → HC uses readout-invisible storage ~5× mHC — [PLSMD](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md).
- Causal test `hc_pos` (HC with every write 2·sigmoid > 0, nothing else changed): predicted gap ~0.10; measured 0.035 (HC 0.048); writes all positive yet zero-sum stream share stays high and ablation dependence is as large as HC (sep +4.19 at c=8) because mixing stays signed → test leaves the cause open — [YAML 15536267](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/logs/pls_depth_controls.yaml); [PAGE Fig 11](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html).

#### Theme F: layer deletion (true 47-layer function; Paloma C4 8×1024)
- In 8 of 10 designs, 13–28 layers of the sep model each cost <0.005 nats to delete vs 0–7 in ordinary models; AttnRes (Block)-sep 3, DeepNorm-sep 8 — [PAGE body after Fig 30](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html).

- DeepNorm-sep's 8 deletable layers are 8 of the 9 flattest-gain layers (all among the deepest 10); flattening a layer's post-norm gains makes removal = deletion; deep profiles are cheap to lose "mainly because they are small, not unused" (downstream sensitivity changes ≤1.5× over layers 31–48 vs a 30–35× fall in relative stream size) — [LEAN PracDeepFlat/PracDeepUse/PracDeepRobust](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/resnext-lean/README.md).
- Ordinary Pre-LN, LNS, DeepNorm, KEEL, mHC, MoDA lean on their last layer (0.32–0.49 nats to delete); with sep the last layer costs ≤0.002 nats — [PAGE](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html).
- Layer 1 costs 2.5–8.6 nats to delete in almost every model; exceptions: ordinary mHC 0.37, ordinary DeepNorm 1.16, ordinary AttnRes (Full) depends most on layer 44 (8.99 nats) — [PAGE](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/plc_site_wt/exps/per-layer-contribution.html). Layer-44 deletion in DEPTHMED attnres-ord gives absolute CE 12.34 (≈ base + 8.99) — [DEPTHMED](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/tmp/pls_blog/depthmed/attnres-ord.json).

#### Theme G: write sizes, DLA, massive activations, effective rank

### Inferences
- The cost is a backbone-allocation effect: heads cannot be improved (Fig 18), stop-grad on heads does not help, and the cost scales with how many layers send gradient (Fig 15 bars 11–13), consistent with "every block must be decodable" competing with features meant for later layers (context circuits form later, Theme A).
- Separate heads appear to reshape the network toward an "early-exit, then nearly idle" profile: middle layers in output coordinates, many deletable layers, no last-MLP correction, aligned AttnRes queries. The ordinary model's late-forming prediction (last quarter, plus a last-MLP anti-correction) is what the separate-heads model gives up.
- HC's half cost and the low HC-sep effective rank (64) plus lowest sep loss may be related (storage in a subspace readouts ignore), but the hc_pos test shows "signed writes" is not the cause; signed mixing remains a candidate.

## Q2. Which results are surprising or unexplained?

### Takeaway
Several anomalies have measurements but no settled mechanism: HC's half last-layer cost (hidden-write account falsified at state level and not confirmed causally), the AttnRes (Full) layer-44 private channel, why separate heads make 13–28 layers nearly free to delete and erase the last-MLP anti-correction, K3's oversized head-avoiding block-end writes, the 1.2b mid-depth next-probe loss under MTP, and the after-next flattening at the top.

- **Dropped/negative results to keep**: gradient cosine as a schedule signal (dead); switch-off oracle gives no lasting gain; 14 fixes fail; MTP gives no gain 130m–1.2b; Lean's two falsified mechanism predictions (Block redundancy fallback; "multiple streams make layer 1 redundant"); trained mHC not close to Pre-LN (read error 0.42–0.48 of √d); oddness separations do not reach realistic inputs (need token norms >1.2e8) — [PLSMD](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/marin-pls/docs/della/pls.md); [LEAN](/scratch/gpfs/GROUP/USER/project/resnext-lean/README.md).

### Inferences
- A common thread may be that separate heads force a single "readable" coordinate system early (lens agreement, query alignment, older-block reads), after which later layers have little to add (deletable, no correction); the ordinary model instead defers decoding and uses late layers (and in AttnRes-Full, a private high-norm channel) for the final readout. This is a synthesis, not a tested claim.
- The HC anomaly may relate to signed mixing (which hc_pos kept) and to multiplicative stream growth (κ up to 5.8e3 ord); neither has been isolated.

### Gaps
- No experiment has tested what the layer-44 source encodes or whether it re-forms under a different seed (Full seed 1 internals were not checked up; only its L48 loss is known).
- No per-design check-up of the seed-1 Full AttnRes model or a second Sandwich seed (declined), so design-level internals rest on single seeds.
- K3 results on WikiText are confounded by memorization (top-1 0.90); the Paloma C4 check-up mitigates this but K3 is still read on only 64×1024 tokens.
